An anchor/pending operation may open its own ancestors. Source-workspace routes also use this same component (`source-bridge.tsx:35–45`, `issues/components/issue-detail.tsx:1169`). Reopen expected disclosures after navigation/reload/popout/restart.
- `execution-panel.tsx:26–58,80–83`: empty execution/history can leave AI execution collapsed or absent for manual/read-only users. Open the visible **AI execution** summary before interacting with Manage AI resources when its fixture has no activity yet. Existing run anchors and unresolved execution intents should open themselves and must keep their original assertions.
- `comment-composer.tsx:68–99,134–140`: **Preview delivery still exists and is supported**. Ordinary Send can obtain preflight and send in one click; explicit AI targets return to visible impact confirmation before a later Send. Changing suppression retires a preview but Send is no longer disabled just because no matching preview exists. Do not bulk delete preview calls or bypass the explicit target confirmation contract.

### Small shared-helper shape and lane boundary

Use real UI interactions in existing fixtures: (1) within Issue actions, if the known-authorized target button is not visible, click the immediate **More actions** summary, then require target visible/enabled and click; (2) locate the exact outer details by its **direct summary text**, click only when closed, then require its panel visible. Reopen after actual remounts. Never use forced clicks, DOM `.open=true`, `includeHidden` clicks, sleeps or looser timeouts to bypass collapsed UI. A helper must reveal a known authorized control and must not decide which actor is authorized.

Keep completion fixture maintenance separate from disclosure changes: it modifies positive setup to comply with the committed exact-result contract, while preserving the original outcome/race/authorization purpose. Request/basis/result/submission IDs must come from real fixture actions/API responses. Do not compare actual capabilities to themselves as the expected policy, replace full role sets by weak contains, or add new product fallbacks.

`fix_check` owns editing fixture/editing-web. A separate disclosure lane can own account-desktop, records-web, delivery-web, knowledge fixture/native source entries and resource helper/direct spec. Completion/default-role/preview-contract files need their own explicit ownership to avoid overlap; workbench and memory are the main semantic cases.
