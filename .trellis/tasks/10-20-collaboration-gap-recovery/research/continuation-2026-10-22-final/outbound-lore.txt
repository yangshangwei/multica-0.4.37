Prevent relayed chat replies from bypassing private collaboration delivery

A relayed WeCom reply could reach its live sender without validating the original task delivery, session, input origin and active workspace. Recheck those facts before text or attachment access; missing or mismatched identity terminates without wire work, while a transient read fault retains the same bounded retry.

Ordinary relay fixtures now carry their real provenance. Local tests cover all existing plugin/IM text, failure, stream, relay, typing and attachment-field exits with private sentinels and ordinary positives. Text-only adapters retain their current capability boundary.
